The stack reads top-down, the way it is composited: the top row is drawn last. The eye switches a layer off without losing its settings; drag a row, or use the arrows, to reorder.

Show draws a layer's resolved mask over the frame. A chroma or luma selector is defined by what it catches in this image, which is not something the YAML can show you.

Every edit rewrites the Scene tab. Ctrl+Z steps back through them.

          Select a region on the plate, then Apply it to the layer
          selected in the Layers tab, or save it as a named region. Hold
          Shift to add to the selection, Alt to take
          away from it, and both to keep only where they overlap — or
          set the mode with the buttons.
        
    
        
    
          Click a region in the list to load it as the selection; change it
          and Update it under the same name. Rename one and
          every layer and region that uses it follows. A region still in
          use, by the scene or by the selection, cannot be deleted.
        
    
        
    
          Marquee (M) drags a box or an oval.
          Lasso (L) draws freehand; Polygon places a
          corner per click; Magnetic finds the edge between your clicks
          by itself. Wand (W) takes the area of one color
          around the click, and Range that color everywhere.
          Pen (P) places a corner per click; drag to
          curve. Brush (B) paints, and erases with
          Alt. Enter closes an outline and
          Esc abandons it.
        
    
        
    
          What is kept is the selector, not the pixels it caught: a wand
          remembers where it was clicked and on what color, and every outline
          is in fractions of the frame, so all of it survives a change of
          width or palette.
        
    
        
    
          An outline is a last resort. A selector keyed on the image itself
          — the wand, the range, or chroma in the Scene tab
          — cuts the exact silhouette of whatever stands in front of the
          region, and keeps doing it when you change the grid.
