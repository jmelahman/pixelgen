        When not to draw one
        
      
          
      
            An outline is a last resort. A selector keyed on the image itself
            cuts the exact silhouette of whatever stands in front of the
            region — a figure, a plant, the window frame — and keeps
            doing it when you change the grid or swap the photograph.
          
      
          
      
            That is what stops rain falling indoors: not a tighter rectangle,
            but chroma, which reads cool daylight apart from a warm
            interior.
